Privacy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

PlatMemoir ("we", "the app") is designed so your photos and location history stay on your device. This policy describes what the app accesses and what we do not collect.

Data on your device

  • Photos library — With your permission, the app reads GPS metadata and thumbnails from photos on this device (including iCloud-synced items) to place pins on the map. Images are not uploaded to PlatMemoir servers.
  • Google Takeout — If you choose to import, you select a folder or archive on your device. Parsing happens locally.
  • Stories, plans, and settings — Stored locally in the app’s database. Handoff backups you export are files you save yourself (e.g. to Files or iCloud Drive).

What we do not do

  • We do not operate a cloud account that stores your photo library.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Exports you create (PDF, ZIP, map snapshots) are shared only when you use the system share sheet.

Third-party services

Map display uses Apple MapKit on device. If you use speech-to-text for stories, recognition may use Apple’s on-device or system speech services according to your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
